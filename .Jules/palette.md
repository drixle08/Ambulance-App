## 2025-05-15 - Medical Safety Confirmation Pattern
**Learning:** In high-pressure clinical tools (like a resuscitation timer), destructive actions like "Reset" must be guarded by a two-step confirmation to prevent accidental data loss. A non-intrusive "Confirm?" label change on the button itself is effective for mobile touch targets.
**Action:** Always implement a debounced or two-step confirmation for critical data reset buttons in clinical UIs.
