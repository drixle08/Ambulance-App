## 2025-05-15 - [Two-step confirmation for destructive actions]
**Learning:** In high-pressure clinical environments, guarding destructive actions (like 'Reset' on a resuscitation timer) with a two-step confirmation pattern reduces the risk of accidental data loss. Using high-contrast visual cues (red background) and haptic feedback on the first tap provides immediate warning.
**Action:** Use a 3-second auto-reverting confirmation state for high-risk clinical buttons.
