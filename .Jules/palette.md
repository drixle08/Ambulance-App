## 2025-05-15 - Guarding Critical Actions in Clinical Tools
**Learning:** In high-pressure clinical environments (like a resuscitation timer), accidental resets can be catastrophic. A two-step "double-tap" confirmation with high-contrast visual feedback (pulsing red) and haptic feedback provides a safe buffer without significantly slowing down the user.
**Action:** Always implement confirmation for destructive or reset actions in clinical timers or documentation logs.
