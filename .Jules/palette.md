## 2025-05-15 - Two-step Confirmation for Resus Timer Reset
**Learning:** In high-stress clinical environments, critical destructive actions (like resetting a timer during a cardiac arrest) must be guarded by a confirmation step to prevent accidental data loss. Visual (color change) and haptic (vibration) feedback are essential to signal the state change to the user.
**Action:** Always implement a two-step confirmation with a clear timeout (e.g., 3 seconds) for destructive actions in clinical tools.
