## 2025-05-15 - [Safety Confirmation in Clinical Tools]
**Learning:** In high-pressure clinical environments (like a resuscitation), destructive actions such as 'Reset' should never be a single-tap operation. A two-step confirmation pattern with haptic feedback and a short auto-revert timeout (3 seconds) provides a safety net without introducing significant friction.
**Action:** Always guard destructive actions in clinical decision-support tools with a 'tap-to-confirm' state and provide high-contrast visual cues (e.g., changing button color to red) during the confirmation window.
